Oath Garden · Where it begins

Our Oath Farm

Before the plate, before the table — the soil. The farm is the first room of the house.

Everything at Oath begins in the ground. The Oath Farm is where we grow what the kitchen cooks and the bar pours — vegetables, herbs, flowers and ferments, raised slowly and without shortcuts.

We work with the season rather than against it. What is ready is what is served; what is not simply waits. It means the menu changes as the garden changes, and that a plate in June is not a plate in December. We think that is the point.

Grown here, harvested the morning it's served.

How we grow

No synthetic sprays, no forcing. We build the soil with compost from our own kitchen, rotate what we plant, and leave room for the wild edges where pollinators live. The farm is small and tended by hand — closer to a garden than a field, which is exactly how we want it.

From farm to table

The distance between where a thing is grown and where it is eaten is measured in steps, not miles. Herbs cut at dawn, greens still cool from the night, flowers on the long table. What we cannot grow ourselves, we source from farmers nearby who work the same way.

To walk the beds, join a harvest morning, or simply eat what the farm gave that day — you are always welcome at the table.
