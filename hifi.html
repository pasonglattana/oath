Oath Garden · The Sound of the House

Our Hi-Fi System

Sound you feel as much as hear. A system built not for volume, but for warmth, presence and the pleasure of listening.

Music is one of the three rituals of the house, and in the garden it moves through the air all day — low in the morning, fuller as the light turns gold. We built the system around analog warmth: valve amplification, vinyl, and speakers chosen for how they fill a room rather than how loud they can go.

The record decides the pace, and so do you.

Nothing here is background wallpaper. Records are played side by side, needle down, no shuffle — the way an album was meant to be heard. On quieter nights, the garden becomes a listening room.
